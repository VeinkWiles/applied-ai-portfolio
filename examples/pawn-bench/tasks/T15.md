Benchmark task T15 (Knight's job: Blender art pipeline, modular character kit). Blender 5.3 runs headless as `blender --background --python <script> -- <args>`.

In <nextimus-pawn>, build the male modular character kit with hair: the base body cut into regions, plus the Ranger and Knight gear and two hair styles as separate pieces, all in one .glb with a manifest. In the game, worn gear shows on the character: a loadout shows its worn pieces and hides the body regions they cover. Read the "Modular kits" section of the blender-rigged-pare skill first.

Sources (15 files, all on the same 65-bone skeleton, all in the same rest pose). They live outside the repo under `<home>/src/art-packs/quaternius/Source/`: read them from there, don't copy them into the repo, don't edit them. The paths have spaces and brackets.

A head piece that covers the whole head hides the hair: `ranger_hood` and `knight_helm` each get `"hides": ["hair"]` in the manifest (a circlet, later, would not). No other piece hides a slot.

What to make:
- `art/scripts/build_chr_male_kit_v002.py`, a Blender script that rebuilds everything from the sources every time it runs. Build it with the house library `<pawn-bench>/tools/pare_lib.py`; don't write your own split, join, material or export code.
- `art/export/chr_male_kit_v002.glb`: one armature with the same 65 bone names, every mesh skinned to it (never re-skin), one material per mesh, and these meshes:
  - the body as five region meshes from `pl.split_regions`: `body_head`, `body_torso`, `body_arms`, `body_legs`, `body_feet`. Together they are the complete body. Don't delete body faces. Decimate the whole body mesh **before** `pl.split_regions` and never decimate a `body_*` region afterwards: the checker tests that the regions still meet edge to edge, with no cracks.
  - `body_eyes` and `body_eyebrows` (always shown).
  - one mesh per gear piece, named as in the table. `ranger_arms` has two materials, so it is two meshes: `ranger_arms` and `ranger_arms_skin`, both listed under the piece `ranger_arms`.
- `art/export/chr_male_kit_v002.json`, the manifest from `pl.write_kit`, with the set, slot and covered regions of each piece exactly as in the table, and `"hides": ["hair"]` on the two head pieces.
- **Budget: every loadout fits in 10,000 triangles and 12 surfaces.** A loadout is the eyes and eyebrows, plus the body regions no worn piece covers, plus none or one piece per slot (hair is a slot), from either set, in any mix; hair is not drawn while a hood or helm is worn. That includes the bare body, full Ranger, full Knight and the heaviest mix. The sources add up to far more than that (the Knight arms alone are 9,144 triangles), so give every mesh a triangle target and add up the heaviest choice per slot before you build. `pl.write_kit` prints the result.
- Keep every piece recognisable: each is checked against its own source for shape, texture, UVs, vertex colours and skin weights. Leave `body_eyes` at its source triangle count.
- **Textures at most 512 px a side:** call `pl.shrink_textures(<the .glb>, 512)` after the export.
- Four previews, 512 px, rendered from the exported .glb with the house script and the manifest (`... render_preview.py -- <glb> <png> 512 kit=<json> wear=<pieces>`):
  - `art/previews/chr_male_kit_v002_bare.png`: `wear=hair_parted` (no gear, first hair style)
  - `art/previews/chr_male_kit_v002_ranger.png`: all six Ranger pieces
  - `art/previews/chr_male_kit_v002_knight.png`: all six Knight pieces
  - `art/previews/chr_male_kit_v002_mix.png`: `wear=hair_ponytail,knight_body,ranger_arms,ranger_legs,ranger_boots` (no head piece, second hair style)
- Report the triangles and surfaces of the bare body, full Ranger, full Knight, the mix and the heaviest loadout, and how you measured them.

The checker for this task:
`python3 <pawn-bench>/tools/kit_check.py art/export/chr_male_kit_v002.glb art/export/chr_male_kit_v002.json --script art/scripts/build_chr_male_kit_v002.py --previews art/previews/chr_male_kit_v002 --kit male-hair`
It takes a few minutes. Send its output to a log file outside the repo and read the PASS/FAIL lines.
