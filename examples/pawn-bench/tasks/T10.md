Benchmark task T10 (Knight's job: Blender art pipeline, assemble a complete character). Blender 5.3 runs headless as `blender --background --python <script> -- <args>`.

In <nextimus-pawn>, build a complete female Wizard (the caster) from three source files and pare it to the character budget. All three share the same 65-bone skeleton and line up in the same rest pose. They live outside the repo under `<home>/src/art-packs/quaternius/Source/`: read them from there, don't copy them into the repo and don't edit anything in that folder. The paths have spaces and brackets, so quote them in shell commands.
- Outfit: `Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy[Source]/Exports/glTF (Godot-Unreal)/Outfits/Female_Wizard.gltf` (about 13,600 triangles; the outfit plus the bare skin of the arms; it has no head).
- Head: `Universal Base Characters[Source]/Base Characters/Exports/Godot - UE/Regular_Female_OnlyHead.gltf` (about 5,500 triangles: head, eyes, eyebrows).
- Hair: `Universal Base Characters[Source]/Hairstyles/Rigged to Head Bone/glTF (Godot -Unreal)/Female/Hair_Long.gltf` (about 2,900 triangles, already skinned to the head bone).

What to make:
- `art/scripts/build_chr_wizard_female_full_v001.py`, a Blender script that rebuilds the result from the three sources every time it runs. Build it with the house library `<pawn-bench>/tools/pare_lib.py` as the skill shows; don't write your own join, split, material or export code.
- `art/export/chr_wizard_female_full_v001.glb` with the whole character (outfit, arms, head, eyes, eyebrows, hair): **at most 10,000 triangles and at most 6 surfaces** (mesh primitives) in total, down from about 22,000 triangles and 11 surfaces.
- **One armature.** The result must have a single armature with the same 65 bone names, and every mesh skinned to it (`pl.load([...])` does this). Don't re-skin.
- **At most four materials, one per source texture:** outfit (`T_Wizard_BaseColor`), skin (`T_Regular_Female_Dark_BaseColor`, shared by the arms and the head), hair (`T_Hair_2_BaseColor`, shared by the hair and the eyebrows) and eyes (`T_Eye_Brown`). Each face stays on the texture it had; every vertex keeps its source UVs and skin weights.
- Decimate only as far as needed and keep every part: nothing visible in the sources may be missing from the export. Spend triangles where they show (face, hair, silhouette).
- Render one three-quarter preview from the front, 512 px, to `art/previews/chr_wizard_female_full_v001_threequarter.png`. The face must be visible. Render it from the exported .glb with the house script, not with your own render code: `blender --background --python <pawn-bench>/tools/render_preview.py -- <the .glb> <the .png> 512`.
- **Textures at most 512 px a side** (ASSETS.md): call `pl.shrink_textures(<the .glb>, 512)` after the export. They must still be the source textures, only smaller.
- Report the final triangle and surface counts, and how you measured them.

The checker takes the extra sources with `--also`:
`python3 <pawn-bench>/tools/glb_check.py <your .glb> "<outfit .gltf>" --also "<head .gltf>" --also "<hair .gltf>" --max-tris 10000 --max-surfaces 6 --preview <your preview .png> --size 512 --max-texture 512 --script <your build script>`

Done when the script runs cleanly from the command line, the .glb and preview exist, the checker prints ALL CHECKS PASS, and you have committed the script, .glb and preview on the branch named below. Long Blender runs: write output to a log file outside the repo and read only the summary.
