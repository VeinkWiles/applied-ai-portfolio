Benchmark task T11 (Knight's job: Blender art pipeline, assemble a new character). Blender 5.3 runs headless as `blender --background --python <script> -- <args>`.

In <nextimus-pawn>, build a complete male Knight from four source files and pare it to the character budget. All four share the same 65-bone skeleton and line up in the same rest pose. They live outside the repo under `<home>/src/art-packs/quaternius/Source/`: read them from there, don't copy them into the repo and don't edit anything in that folder. The paths have spaces and brackets, so quote them in shell commands.
- Outfit: `Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy[Source]/Exports/glTF (Godot-Unreal)/Outfits/Male_Knight.gltf` (about 25,200 triangles in 7 armour parts; it has no head).
- Head: `Universal Base Characters[Source]/Base Characters/Exports/Godot - UE/Regular_Male_OnlyHead.gltf` (about 4,800 triangles: head, eyes, eyebrows).
- Hair: `Universal Base Characters[Source]/Hairstyles/Rigged to Head Bone/glTF (Godot -Unreal)/Male/Hair_Balding.gltf` (about 1,700 triangles).
- Beard: `Universal Base Characters[Source]/Hairstyles/Rigged to Head Bone/glTF (Godot -Unreal)/Male/Hair_Beard.gltf` (about 1,000 triangles).

What to make:
- `art/scripts/build_chr_knight_male_full_v001.py`, a Blender script that rebuilds the result from the four sources every time it runs. Build it with the house library `<pawn-bench>/tools/pare_lib.py` as the skill shows; don't write your own join, split, material or export code.
- **Leave the helmet off.** The outfit's `Male_Knight_Head_Armet` would hide the face, hair and beard. Remove that one part (`pl.remove`) and keep everything else: both pauldrons, scarf, arms, body armour, legs and feet.
- `art/export/chr_knight_male_full_v001.glb` with the whole character (armour without the helmet, head, eyes, eyebrows, hair, beard): **at most 10,000 triangles and at most 6 surfaces** (mesh primitives) in total, down from about 31,000 triangles. This is a harder cut than usual, about one triangle in three kept. The arms are by far the densest part (about 9,100 triangles); plan where the triangles go so the face, beard and silhouette stay readable and no part collapses.
- **One armature** with the same 65 bone names, every mesh skinned to it. Don't re-skin.
- **At most four materials, one per source texture:** armour (`T_Knight_BaseColor`), skin (`T_Regular_Male_Dark_BaseColor`), hair (`T_Hair_1_BaseColor`, shared by the hair, beard and eyebrows) and eyes (`T_Eye_Brown`). Each face stays on the texture it had; every vertex keeps its source UVs, vertex colours and skin weights.
- **Textures at most 512 px a side** (ASSETS.md): call `pl.shrink_textures(<the .glb>, 512)` after the export. They must still be the source textures, only smaller.
- Nothing visible in the sources may be missing from the export, apart from the helmet.
- Render one three-quarter preview, 512 px, to `art/previews/chr_knight_male_full_v001_threequarter.png`, from the exported .glb with the house script: `blender --background --python <pawn-bench>/tools/render_preview.py -- <the .glb> <the .png> 512`. The face and beard must be visible.
- Report the final triangle and surface counts, and how you measured them.

The checker for this task:
`python3 <pawn-bench>/tools/glb_check.py <your .glb> "<outfit .gltf>" --skip Male_Knight_Head_Armet --also "<head .gltf>" --also "<hair .gltf>" --also "<beard .gltf>" --max-tris 10000 --max-surfaces 6 --preview <your preview .png> --size 512 --max-texture 512 --script <your build script>`

Done when the script runs cleanly from the command line, the .glb and preview exist, the checker prints ALL CHECKS PASS, and you have committed the script, .glb and preview on the branch named below. Long Blender runs: write output to a log file outside the repo and read only the summary.
