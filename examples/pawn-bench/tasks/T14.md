In the game, worn gear shows on the character: a loadout shows its worn pieces and hides the body regions they cover. Read the "Modular kits" section of the blender-rigged-pare skill first.

What to make:
- `art/scripts/build_chr_female_kit_v001.py`, a Blender script that rebuilds everything from the sources every time it runs. Build it with the house library `<pawn-bench>/tools/pare_lib.py`; don't write your own split, join, material or export code.
- `art/export/chr_female_kit_v001.glb`: one armature with the same 65 bone names, every mesh skinned to it (never re-skin), one material per mesh, and these meshes:
  - the body as five region meshes from `pl.split_regions`: `body_head`, `body_torso`, `body_arms`, `body_legs`, `body_feet`. Together they are the complete body. Don't delete body faces. Decimate the whole body mesh **before** `pl.split_regions` and never decimate a `body_*` region afterwards: the checker tests that the regions still meet edge to edge, with no cracks.
  - `body_eyes` and `body_eyebrows` (always shown).
  - one mesh per gear piece, named as in the table. `ranger_arms` has two materials, so it is two meshes: `ranger_arms` (sleeves and bracer merged) and `ranger_arms_skin` (the hands), both listed under the piece `ranger_arms`. A piece whose source has several meshes on one material (`ranger_body`, `knight_arms`) is one merged mesh.
- `art/export/chr_female_kit_v001.json`, the manifest from `pl.write_kit`, with the set, slot and covered regions of each piece exactly as in the table.
- **Budget: every loadout fits in 10,000 triangles and 12 surfaces.** A loadout is the eyes and eyebrows, plus the body regions no worn piece covers, plus none or one piece per slot, from either set, in any mix. That includes the bare body, full Ranger, full Knight and the heaviest mix. The sources add up to far more than that (the Ranger boots alone are 9,172 triangles), so give every mesh a triangle target and add up the heaviest choice per slot before you build. `pl.write_kit` prints the result.
- Keep every piece recognisable: each is checked against its own source for shape, texture, UVs, vertex colours and skin weights. Leave `body_eyes` at its source triangle count.
- **Textures at most 512 px a side:** call `pl.shrink_textures(<the .glb>, 512)` after the export.
- Four previews, 512 px, rendered from the exported .glb with the house script and the manifest (`... render_preview.py -- <glb> <png> 512 kit=<json> wear=<pieces>`):
  - `art/previews/chr_female_kit_v001_bare.png`: `wear=` (nothing worn)
  - `art/previews/chr_female_kit_v001_ranger.png`: all six Ranger pieces
  - `art/previews/chr_female_kit_v001_knight.png`: all six Knight pieces
  - `art/previews/chr_female_kit_v001_mix.png`: `wear=knight_helm,knight_body,ranger_arms,ranger_legs,ranger_boots`
- Report the triangles and surfaces of the bare body, full Ranger, full Knight, the mix and the heaviest loadout, and how you measured them.
